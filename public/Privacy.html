Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What we collect

Name, email address, and activity data (challenge attempts, test scores). Email is stored encrypted.

How we use it

Only to operate the app — tracking progress and showing instructors their students' activity. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Who can see your data

Your instructor can see your scores and attempt history within their classroom. No one else.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can request full deletion at any time.

Your rights (FERPA)

You have the right to export all your data or delete your account. To do so, contact your instructor or email us directly.
