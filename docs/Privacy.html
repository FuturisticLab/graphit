Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

Display name, selected role (student or instructor), and activity data (challenge attempts, test scores, and timestamps). We do not collect email addresses, passwords, or any personally identifiable information (PII).

How we use it

Only to operate the app — tracking progress, displaying classroom rosters, auditing coordinate attempts, and updating rankings. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Who can see your data

Your instructor can see your scores, display name, and attempt history within their managed classroom dashboard. No one else has access to this data.

How long we keep it

Until you choose to delete your profile. In guest/serverless mode, data persists only in your browser storage. In full-stack mode, you can request full profile deletion instantly from your settings panel.

Your rights (FERPA)

You have the absolute right to export all your activity records or delete your account at any time directly through the profile settings interface. You also have the right to request data audits from your instructor.
